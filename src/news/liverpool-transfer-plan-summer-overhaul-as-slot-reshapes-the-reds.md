Liverpool are poised for a transformative summer under manager Arne Slot, with significant moves both in and out of Anfield.

### 🎯 Wirtz Set to Become Record Signing

The Reds are reportedly prepared to meet Bayer Leverkusen's £126 million asking price for Florian Wirtz, aiming to expedite the deal and avoid drawn-out negotiations. The 22-year-old German midfielder has reportedly agreed to a five-year contract, with final details expected to be concluded soon. Wirtz recently impressed by scoring in Germany’s Nations League semi-final against Portugal, further strengthening Liverpool's determination to secure his signature.

### 🔄 Nunez's Future in Doubt

Darwin Nunez’s future at Anfield appears uncertain. Signed for a club-record £85 million in 2022, the Uruguayan striker has struggled to live up to expectations. Reports indicate that Arne Slot has informed Nunez he is not part of the club’s long-term plans. Interest is said to be coming from European giants such as Barcelona, as well as clubs in the Saudi Pro League.

### 🛡️ Defensive Reinforcements

Alongside their pursuit of Wirtz, Liverpool are closing in on a £45 million deal for 21-year-old Bournemouth left-back Milos Kerkez. The Hungarian international is expected to provide strong competition for Andy Robertson. A five-year deal is reportedly already in place, with an official announcement anticipated soon.

### 🚀 Slot's Vision for Liverpool

Arne Slot’s remarkable debut season, capped by a Premier League title, has paved the way for an ambitious summer rebuild. With marquee signings like Wirtz and Kerkez in the pipeline and the possible departure of Nunez, Slot is shaping a new era at Anfield—one built for long-term success.
