In a move that could shake up Real Madrid's transfer strategy, manager Xabi Alonso is reportedly considering activating an €8 million buyback clause to re-sign midfielder Nico Paz from Como. According to transfer expert Fabrizio Romano, the decision is expected imminently.

Paz, who initially left Madrid due to limited first-team opportunities, has shown promise at Como. While he may not yet be ready for a starting role at the Bernabéu, Real Madrid's interest could be strategic, potentially aiming to develop or profit from his future transfers.

Alonso, fresh off an unbeaten season with Bayer Leverkusen, is keen to blend youth with experience as he shapes Madrid's next era. Reacquiring Paz could be a calculated step in that direction.
