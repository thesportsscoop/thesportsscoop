The summer transfer window is heating up with a flurry of activity as clubs across Europe look to strengthen their squads. Here's a rundown of the most compelling whispers and confirmed movements:

* **Ramsdale to Newcastle?** Newcastle United is reportedly in advanced discussions to bring goalkeeper Aaron Ramsdale from Southampton to St. James' Park.
* **Bednarek Joins Porto:** Defender Jan Bednarek has made a permanent switch to Porto from Southampton in a deal worth £6.5 million.
* **Everton Eye Grealish Loan; Man Utd Considers Donnarumma:** Everton is reportedly exploring a loan agreement for Manchester City's Jack Grealish, while Manchester United is weighing a potential move for renowned goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma.

* **Millwall Secures Doughty:** Millwall has officially announced the signing of Alfie Doughty from Luton Town on a long-term deal.
* **Ndoye's Forest Price Tag Revealed:** Nottingham Forest's pursuit of Bologna's Dan Ndoye could cost them around £34 million, as discussions continue.
* **Liverpool Eyeing Isak After Diaz Sale?** Rumours suggest Liverpool is preparing to offload Luis Diaz to Bayern Munich, potentially paving the way for a move for Newcastle forward Alexander Isak.

* **Sporting Targets Forest Winger:** Sporting Lisbon has reportedly held talks concerning the acquisition of Nottingham Forest's Jota Silva, preferring a loan with a buy option.
* **Everton's Interest in Maitland-Niles:** Former Arsenal midfielder Ainsley Maitland-Niles is reportedly on Everton's radar.

* **Brownhill Confirms Burnley Exit:** Josh Brownhill has announced his departure from Burnley as his contract concludes.
